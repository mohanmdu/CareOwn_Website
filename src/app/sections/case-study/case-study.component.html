Case Study

Built & Running: MC Hospital

An early look at what a full CareOwn HMS deployment looks like in practice.

A multi-specialty hospital's full digital front door

MC Hospital runs on CareOwn HMS end to end — patient registration and queueing at the front desk, OP/IP billing, pharmacy and lab, and a CMS-managed public website where patients book appointments without ever calling in.

  • Live token queue with a waiting-room display board
  • Consultation-to-billing handoff, no manual re-entry
  • Public website content managed entirely from the admin console
